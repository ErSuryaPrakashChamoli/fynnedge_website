# 13 — Operational user guide (advisors)

## Daily
1. **Dashboard**: check new enquiries, follow-ups due and expiring quotations.
2. **Enquiries**: call each new enquirer. Open the case → **Change status → Contacted**. Help them add their loans (they can do it themselves from the link they received or from My Profile), or add them yourself in **Existing liabilities**.

## Working a case
1. **Verify the loans.** Ask for statements (the customer uploads in My Profile, or you upload in **Documents**). Open each document, then **Verify** (or **Reject** with a reason the customer will see). In **Existing liabilities** use **Verify** on each loan and correct figures with **Edit** — every change is in the audit history.
2. **Foreclosure letters.** Record each lender's quotation (**Record quotation**), attach the letter, then **Verify** it in Foreclosure Analysis. Quotations expire — watch the dashboard.
3. **Recalculate.** Check **Financial assessment**, **Suitability score**, **Savings comparison** and **Foreclosure analysis**. Read every warning. If consolidating costs more, tell the customer so and close the case as "Not proceeding" with the reason.
4. **Match lenders.** Read the reasons for each lender; fill in missing information; mark lenders **Shortlisted** or **Not suitable** with your reasoning. Shortlisted lenders appear in the customer's profile with a consent box.
5. **Consent, then apply.** Only after the customer consents to a lender (in their profile, or recorded by you with evidence) can you open a **New lender application**. Record every update the lender gives you with **Record lender update** — approvals need a note of how the lender confirmed it; disbursement needs the amount and date.
6. **Close the old loans.** Disbursement opens a closure tracker per loan. Make sure the amount to pay is known: for a full closure, the lender's quotation (recorded at any time); for a part-payment, enter the final closure amount. Record payments (**Record payment**), **Confirm** them only with a UTR or proof (payments must cover the amount), request closure, upload the NOC as an **NOC / closure letter** for that loan and verify it, then **Update closure → Closure verified**. A case in "Closure in progress" completes automatically when every closure is verified, and the customer is told. A loan added after disbursement is not part of that financing: set it to **Exclude**.
7. **Reports.** **Generate report** (choose the type; tick "Release" for customer reports). The internal advisor assessment is never released.

## Never
- Describe a match, score or scenario as an approval or offer.
- Mark a loan closed without confirmed payment and (full closure) a verified NOC.
- Share a customer's details with a lender before their consent to that lender.
